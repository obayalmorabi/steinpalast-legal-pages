Privacy Policy

Steinpalast Social Publisher

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. About the Application

Steinpalast Social Publisher is an internal social media management tool operated on behalf of Steinpalast. It allows authorized team members to prepare, review, and publish content to connected social media accounts, including TikTok.

2. Data We Process

Depending on the permissions granted and features used, the application may process:

  • TikTok account identifiers and basic profile information.
  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens.
  • Videos, images, captions, and publishing instructions.
  • Publishing status, timestamps, and related technical logs.

3. How Data Is Used

Data is processed to authenticate authorized accounts, prepare and publish social media content, check publishing results, and maintain the application's operation and security.

4. TikTok Integration

The application uses TikTok's official APIs where authorized. The permissions requested may include user.info.basic, video.publish, and video.upload.

Content is published only following an authorized publishing action. TikTok processes information according to its own privacy policies.

5. Data Sharing

Information required for publishing may be transferred to the connected social media platform. The application is not intended to sell personal data.

6. Storage and Security

Application data and authentication credentials must be protected against unauthorized access. Access is restricted to authorized personnel and necessary service providers. Temporary media and operational records should be retained only as long as required for the applicable purpose.

7. Legal Basis and Retention

Personal data is processed according to applicable data protection law, including the GDPR where relevant. Specific legal bases and retention periods depend on the processing activity and applicable legal obligations.

8. Your Rights

Where applicable, individuals may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, or other rights available under applicable law.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's functions or data-processing practices change.
